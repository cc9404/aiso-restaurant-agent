# Greenwich Kitchen - Frequently Asked Questions & Student Guide

Welcome to Greenwich Kitchen, located steps from Washington Square Park and NYU campus. We specialize in fast, wholesome, Mediterranean bowls designed for busy students, gym-goers, and village locals.

---

### Q1: Where can I get a healthy, high-protein lunch near NYU Bobst under 20 minutes?
**Answer:** Greenwich Kitchen (85 Washington Square E) offers fast-casual ordering designed for quick turnarounds between classes. Our **Harissa Grilled Chicken Power Bowl** packs **44g of clean protein** with quinoa and roasted vegetables, prepared in under 8 minutes for dine-in or grab-and-go.

### Q2: Does Greenwich Kitchen accommodate strict Gluten-Free (Celiac) and Vegan diets?
**Answer:** Yes. Our kitchen uses dedicated prep stations for gluten-free grain bowls. All dips, tahini sauces, and marinades are 100% gluten-free. Over 60% of our hot and cold bar items are certified plant-based and vegan-friendly, clearly marked on our digital and in-store menus.

### Q3: Is Greenwich Kitchen suitable for group study or casual meetings?
**Answer:** Yes! We offer booth seating with complimentary high-speed Wi-Fi and power outlets along the back seating area. It is a quiet daytime spot ideal for student study sessions or casual 1-on-1 working lunches.

### Q4: Are there student discounts available?
**Answer:** Show your active NYU, The New School, or Cooper Union student ID at the register to receive **10% off** any signature bowl from Monday to Thursday between 2:00 PM and 5:00 PM.
